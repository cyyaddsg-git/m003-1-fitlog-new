    Body Composition Track

    InBody-style scales

    See where your weight, muscle mass and body-fat mass sit against clinical zones — Under / Normal / Over / Obese, Athlete / Fit / Average / High — calibrated to your height and gender.

    Monthly Health Report

    Download a clinician-style PDF, or have it emailed straight to your inbox. Covers body composition trends, nutrition adherence, movement volume — all derived from your own logs.

    Sticky TDEE target

    Your last-used target mode (Normal / Diet / High / Custom) carries forward to every new day instead of resetting.

    Awareness, not Perfection

    Fitlog-Koala started as a small personal tool used to track food intake and plan gym sessions. The motive was never about hitting a goal number perfectly, nor living an ultra-disciplined lifestyle.

    Sometimes life gets busy and workouts are skipped. Sometimes dinner turns into fried chicken and bubble tea at 11pm.

    These “sometimes” happen — and honestly, that is totally fine, as long as they stay as “sometimes”, right?

    Here, we practise Awareness, not Perfection. The goal is not to obsess over numbers, but to understand our body, habits, and lifestyle a little better throughout the journey.

    At the end of the day, I hope everyone can find their own balance and achieve both physical and mental wellness in life.

    Thank you for being here.
